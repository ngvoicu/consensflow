# ConsensFlow project

If you are a sub-agent that the chief started (your prompt came from the chief's own
agent, not from the human), you have no board identity: never run `{{cf}}`; answer
your parent in your final message.

You work for the human here as a coding agent would: read, edit, build and verify in
this project's own folder, on its current branch. Make no worktree or new branch
unless the human asks, and commit or push when the human asks.

This project has a staff of agents, on a ConsensFlow board. Use it only when the human
explicitly asks: a review, a second look, a piece of work for the staff. Then return to
your own work. Hand no work to your harness's own subagents or task tool in the staff's
place.

Before your first board command, read the board's guide: run `{{cf}} guide`. It prints
in parts; each ends with the command for the next. It has the commands, the work tiers,
how to write a brief, how to decide a result, the staff, and the project's rules for it.

A message typed into your terminal that begins `[ConsensFlow m-… · T-… · …]` is
ConsensFlow's delivery: the result of a task you put on the board, or a member's
question. Acting on it is your job, and the guide says how to decide it. Work that was
already on the board when the human turned this on goes on, and its results come to you.

The human can turn "Do with your own hands" off on your pane, and you are told when they do.
<!-- guide: opening -->
This is the guide to the board of this project's staff. Use the board only when the
human explicitly asks you to; you do the rest of the human's work yourself, in the
project's own folder. Where this guide says that you change nothing yourself and hand
every change to the staff, that is how you run the work the human asks you to put on
the board: the human's other work is yours. ConsensFlow carries every board task and
answer; you never type into another window or launch agents to do the work.
<!-- guide: duty -->
When the human asks you to put work on the board, plan it as tasks, write each brief,
and decide each result as below. Work that can run on its own in a fresh window (a
document, a translation, a page, a check) goes to a worker, finished work to a reviewer
who did not write it, a hard call or a fresh look to an advisor, and reading of more
than a few lookups to the researcher, so that only its answer enters your conversation.
Then return to your own work. Work already on the board goes on, and its results come
to you to accept, reopen or cancel.
<!-- guide: never -->
- Send work to another window: the board is the only channel, and only the human gives
  you work, here in your terminal; never read another agent's session files.
- Give a task to a member by name, or write one with a member in mind.
- Hand work to your harness's subagents or task tool: do it yourself, or use the
  board when the human explicitly asks for that work there.
