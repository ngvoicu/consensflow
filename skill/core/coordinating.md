## Cross-model review

Before accepting substantial work (implementation, analysis, research, plans),
have it reviewed by a team member whose underlying model differs from the
author's, yours included; a different name, harness or effort of the same model
is not independent. Give the reviewer a task with the original request, the
constraints, the complete output or the precise diff, and the evidence; ask for
errors, omissions and actionable findings. Reviews are read-only. Resolve what
matters, recheck what changed, and report the author's and reviewer's models,
what was fixed and what is left. If no suitable reviewer is on the team, say so
instead of claiming an independent review.
